# Open questions

These are the questions the weekend should answer. None of them is decided. Open an issue per question when you start on it, and close it with a *Decision* issue when the team agrees.

## Positioning
- A note-taker that also acts, or "a teammate / PM on your calls"?
- What one sentence beats *"Otter does that"*?
- Speaking in calls is becoming common. What stays Octo's?

## Who first (ICP)
- Which team feels the pain every week and would buy without a sales call?
- See the starting guesses in [hypotheses.md](hypotheses.md).

## Brand
- "Octo" as the brand, or each customer's own named agent by default?
- Is the custom name and voice a feature, or the default?

## Price and model
- Note-taker band ($8–30 per seat), platform-agent band (~$20 per user plus credits), or per team / workspace?
- Free tier, trial, or neither?

## Channel and growth loop
- Can every call be the growth loop? What does a guest get from seeing Octo?
- Integration marketplaces (Zoom, Slack, Notion, Asana, Jira)?
- Agencies as resellers?
- Content and SEO comparisons?

## Trust
- How do guests consent to an agent that speaks?
- What does Octo never do? Write it down as a promise.
