# Workstreams

Pick one, add your name, and put your output in `work/<folder>/`. These are suggestions: merge, split or drop them as the team sees fit.

| Workstream | Folder | Question it answers | Output by the final pitch |
|---|---|---|---|
| Positioning & ICP | `work/positioning/` | Who do we sell to first, and what's the one-liner? | One positioning statement, one ICP, and why (with interview notes) |
| Growth loop | `work/growth/` | How does each call bring the next customer? | The loop on one page, the first experiment, and the metric |
| Brand & design | `work/brand/` | Octo or a named agent? What does it look and sound like? | Name decision, logo or wordmark direction, key visual |
| Landing page | `work/landing/` | What would the first visitor see and click? | A live or clickable landing page with a sign-up / waitlist |
| Content & video | `work/content/` | What makes people stop scrolling? | A 30-second clip or post, plus the launch copy |
| Pricing | `work/pricing/` | Seat, team or usage? Free tier? | The price page draft and the reasoning |

## Suggested rhythm

1. **Kick-off.** Everyone reads [`README`](../README.md), watches the demo, and picks a workstream.
2. **Mid-point check-in.** Each workstream shows what it has in 2 minutes. Kill what isn't working.
3. **Final.** Assemble one pitch from the outputs: positioning, loop, brand, landing, clip, price.

Fill in the real times from the hackathon schedule.
