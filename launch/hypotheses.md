# Starting hypotheses (to break)

Rough guesses from Oleksii. The point of the weekend is to test, replace or kill them.

## Who first

1. **Remote agencies and studios, 10–50 people.**
   - Many client and team calls, on mixed platforms.
   - They live in ClickUp, Asana or Trello.
   - Pain: tasks die after calls.
2. **Startup product and engineering teams on Jira or Linear.**
   - The tracker is the source of truth.
   - Daily standups.
   - The weekly review and the tracker that updates itself replace status meetings.
3. **Founder-led small businesses with no project manager.**
   - Octo as "the PM who never misses a follow-up".

## Growth loops to test

- **Every guest sees Octo.**
  - The bot in the meeting worked for Fireflies and Read.ai.
  - What does the guest get? Their own task message? A one-click "get Octo for your team" link?
- **The task DM is the invite.**
  - Each person gets value, not just the buyer.
  - The per-person message with the exact quote is shareable proof.
- **Integration marketplaces.**
  - Zoom App Marketplace, Slack, Notion, ClickUp, Asana and Jira directories.
  - Fathom got 100k sign-ups in a month from Zoom, but few stayed.
- **The launch spike.**
  - Product Hunt.
  - A 30-second clip of Octo updating a tracker live, for socials.

## How to test something this weekend

- Five quick interviews with people who match an ICP: "When did a task last die after a call?"
- A landing page per positioning, and a sign-up button to see which line people click.
- A short clip posted to a relevant community, to see which hook gets replies.
