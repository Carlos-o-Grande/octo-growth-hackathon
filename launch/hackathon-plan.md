# 3-hour plan: six things, four people

The brief: ship **Plan, Landing page, Targeting & campaign, Social, Paid, Bold idea** in three hours. Only the Grona work goes live, so that's the landing page and the targeting.

## Decided at ~14:40 (this overrides the defaults below)

- **ICP:** small teams of up to 50 people with no project manager. Remote agencies are the slice we name.
- **Positioning:** "For founders and ops leaders at remote agencies, Octo is a teammate that joins every client and team call on whatever platform the client picks. It answers when you call it by name. Afterwards, each person gets their own tasks with exact quotes and deadlines, and ClickUp or Asana stays up to date until the work is done."
- **Model:** self-serve SaaS for small teams, not enterprise sales. Octo as a junior project manager.
- **One campaign, one main channel:** "Who's doing what?" on **Meta ads** (vertical video). Second channel: **YouTube creators** with sponsored segments. LinkedIn is organic, for A/B testing one-liners.
- **Comparison with note-takers** (Granola, Fireflies, Otter, Fathom) goes on the landing page.

## Defaults, so the first 20 minutes don't become 60

- **Audience:** heads of ops and founders at remote agencies, 10–50 people. Many client calls, on mixed platforms. Their pain: tasks die after calls.
- **One-liner:** "Note-takers record the meeting. Octo takes part in it."
- **Hook:** "Every call ends with: so who's doing what?"
- **CTA:** "Get early access" (book a demo).
- **Proof:** the 36-second demo video, 16:9 and 9:16.

Change any of them in the first 20 minutes, then freeze.

## €1,000: a starting split for the plan

| Channel | € | Why |
|---|---|---|
| LinkedIn ads | 400 | Job title and company size reach agency owners and ops leads |
| Meta / Instagram Reels | 250 | The 9:16 demo is the creative; retarget page visitors |
| Google Search | 200 | Intent: "AI meeting assistant", "Otter alternative", "meeting notes to tasks" |
| Test budget | 150 | Reddit or newsletter test; keep it for the best-performing hook |

## Bold idea: candidates (pick one)

1. **The demo is run by the product.** The CTA books a call where Octo itself shows the demo, answers questions and books the follow-up. "Talk to Octo, not a sales rep." *(Check with Oleksii first: Octo has fewer abilities with outside guests.)*
2. **"Fire your note-taker" week.** Send us your last meeting's AI summary. We show what Octo would have *done* with it: tasks, owners, updates.
3. **Every guest gets their own task.** Guests on any Octo call get their action items by DM, plus "want this for your team?" Every call becomes a channel.
