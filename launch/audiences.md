# Audiences (for targeting and ads)

Pick **one primary**, use the rest as tests. The recommendation is at the bottom.

| # | Audience | Their pain | Where and how to reach them | Hook |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| 1 | **Remote agencies, 10–50 people**: founders, managing directors, heads of ops, delivery and account leads, PMs | Many client calls; what the client asked for gets lost before it reaches ClickUp / Asana | **LinkedIn:** those titles; industries Marketing & Advertising, Design, IT Services; size 11–50. **Meta:** interests ClickUp, Asana, Monday.com, agency owners | "Your client said it on the call. Did it make it into ClickUp?" |
| 2 | **Startup product and engineering leads** on Jira / Linear: Head of Product, Engineering Manager, CTO, startups of 11–200 | Standups and syncs; the tracker is always a day behind | **LinkedIn:** those titles, size 11–200, Software / Internet. **Google:** "standup notes to Jira", "Linear meeting notes" | "The standup ends. Jira is already updated." |
| 3 | **Founder-led small businesses, no PM**: CEO / founder, 2–50 people | The founder is the only one following up | **Meta:** small business owners, interests Notion, Trello, productivity. **LinkedIn:** Founder / CEO, size 2–50 | "The PM who never misses a follow-up." |
| 4 | **Note-taker switchers**: already pay for Otter / Fireflies / Fathom and want more | The summary arrives, and nobody acts on it | **Google Search:** "Otter alternative", "Fireflies alternative", "Fathom vs", "AI meeting assistant that creates tasks" | "Your note-taker writes it down. Octo does it." |
| 5 | **Ops at remote-first companies, 50–200**: COO, Chief of Staff, Head of Operations | Too many meetings, no accountability across teams and platforms | **LinkedIn:** those titles, size 51–200, remote-first. **Meta:** lookalike of #1 | "Meetings that finish themselves." |
| 6 | **Retargeting:** landing visitors, 50%+ video viewers | Interested, but didn't sign up | **Meta + LinkedIn** retargeting, 9:16 demo | "Still taking notes by hand? Get early access." |

## Recommendation

- **Primary: #1, remote agencies.** It matches the plan's defaults. The pain is weekly, they run calls on mixed platforms, and the owner buys without procurement.
- **Second: #4, on Google Search.** People already looking for a note-taker alternative: the cheapest intent.
- **Always on: #6, retargeting** for whoever visits the page or watches the video.

How that maps to the €1,000: LinkedIn €400 → #1 · Meta €250 → #1 interests + #6 · Google €200 → #4 · Test €150 → #2 or #3, whichever hook wins.
