# Rough landscape (to verify)

Light notes, gathered on 7 Nov 2026. Prices are list prices per user per month (annual / monthly). **[3p]** means only third-party sources were found: check the vendor page before quoting.

## A. AI note-takers

Record → summary → action items. Mostly passive.

| Product | Speaks in the call? | Acts after the call? | Price ballpark | Growth motion |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| Otter [1][2][3] | Yes: "Hey Otter" Meeting Agent (Zoom first, 03.2025) | Summaries, action items, follow-up email drafts, CRM sync | Pro $8.33 / $16.99; Business $19.99 / $30 | Freemium, bot in meetings; $100M ARR (03.2025) |
| Fireflies [4][5][6] | Yes: "Hey Fireflies" voice/chat, live web search; muted unless triggered | Task detection, integrations | Pro ~$10 / $18; Business ~$19 / $29 [3p] | The bot every guest sees is the viral loop |

| Granola [11] | No (no bot, device audio) | Notes into Notion / Attio / HubSpot | Business $14 | Bot-free, consumer-grade UX |
| tl;dv, Fellow, Avoma, Bluedot [12][13][14] | Not found | Notes, CRM / tool sync | Fellow $7–15; Avoma ~$19+; others vary [3p] | Freemium, SEO comparison content |

## B. Agents that take part in the call

- **Otter Meeting Agent** and **Talk to Fireflies**: voice Q&A on demand (above).
- **Recall.ai Output Media API** (2024): infrastructure that lets any startup's bot speak and show video in Zoom, Meet, Teams and Webex. Speaking in calls is becoming a commodity [15][16].
- **Small 2026 entrants:** TruGen AI, Relly, Koov, MeetGeek voice agents, open-source AgentCall. All vendor claims, unverified [17].

## C. Built into the platforms

- **Microsoft Teams Facilitator** (Copilot licence): notes, agenda, tasks synced to Planner (preview) [18][19].
- **Zoom AI Companion 3.0:** free with paid Zoom, agentic retrieval [20]. **ZoomMate** (06.2026): paid "AI teammate", ~$20/user, prep before and follow-up after; North America first [21][22][23].
- **Gemini in Meet**, "Take notes for me": summary and action items into a Doc [24][25].
- **Notion AI Meeting Notes:** Business plan, ~$20/user [26].

## An honest read

**Where Octo may differ** (hypotheses to test with users):
- One agent across six platforms, including Slack Huddles, Webex and GoTo. Platform tools stay inside their own platform.
- Closes the loop in the team's own tracker: writes tasks, and updates them when someone says "done" on a later call. Each person gets their tasks by DM, with the source quote. Weekly task review by voice.
- Answers from the team's knowledge base, CRM and tracker by voice, and shows it on its tile. Custom name and voice.

**Where it doesn't differ:**
- Joining calls, transcripts, summaries, action items and pre-call briefs are table stakes.
- Speaking on a wake word: Otter and Fireflies already ship it, Fireflies for free.
- "An agent that acts after the call": ZoomMate, Teams Facilitator and Otter are all moving there, with distribution Octo doesn't have.

**The open question:** speaking is becoming common. What stays Octo's?
