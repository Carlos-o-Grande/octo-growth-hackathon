## 1. What Octo can and cannot do (for copy, ads, creators and the landing)

| Octo can, today | Octo cannot, or not yet |
|---|---|
| Join Google Meet, Teams, Zoom, Slack Huddles, Webex and GoTo calls | Self-serve sign-up (our team sets it up) |
| Join the calendar events you choose, on its own | Be invisible to guests on the call |
| Speak when called by name ("Octo, ...") *(confirm whether it stays silent otherwise: the stage demo notes say it answers everything it hears)* | Run a sales-copilot side panel (agenda, what to ask, sentiment): not built |
| Answer from your knowledge base and CRM, search the web, show answers on its own tile | Translate calls |
| Book calendar events after you confirm | Answer "think deeply" questions fast (20 to 60 seconds) |
| Brief before the call: who's attending, last discussion, what's open | Work at full power when an outside guest joins (reduced client mode) |
| Each person's tasks by DM, with a deadline and the exact quote | Plan or prioritise the project for you |
| Tasks into Notion, Asana, Trello, ClickUp (Jira said verbally) and updated when someone says "done" on a later call | Show a public price |
| Update the CRM and Notion from the transcript (Oleksii, 8 Nov: "we just speak and discuss and then all the information in our Notion, in our CRM is being updated") | Show every label in English yet |

Sources: [`product/overview.md`](../../product/overview.md), [`product/live-demo.md`](../../product/live-demo.md), [`pitch/faq.md`](../../pitch/faq.md), Oleksii in the team discussion.

## 2. Wording risks found in the research

- **"Bot" in a hook.** In 34 verbatim customer quotes, consent and bot fatigue are the top theme (19 of 34). People use "bot" as the insult ("I have to kick several of these AI notetakers from our meetings", Zoom Community). Creative 1's hook "This meeting bot talks back." may trigger it. Alternative to test: "This meeting tool talks back." or "Ask your meeting a question." ([`voice-of-customer.md`](../../growth-kit/outputs/research/voice-of-customer.md))
- **"AI teammate".** ZoomMate ("Meet ZoomMate, your AI teammate") and Fireflies ("AI Teammate & Note Taker") use it on their pages. It does not separate Octo. ([`competitor-teardown.md`](../../growth-kit/outputs/research/competitor-teardown.md))
- **"AI meeting agent".** Sembly ("the AI meeting agent", for professional services) and Otter own it.
- **Naming competitors in a paid ad** (creative 3). Comparative ads are allowed in the EU if fair and verifiable, but Meta may limit delivery and the brands can complain. Safer: "Your note-taker" without names in the ad, names only on the comparison page.
- **"Joins only when invited"**: false. Octo joins the calendar events you pick on its own. Use "You choose which calls it joins."

## 3. One more angle worth a creative: "nobody types"

Oleksii's own words are the strongest proof we have: "we're not writing anything, we just speak and discuss and then all the information in our Notion, in our CRM is being updated." It covers tasks, CRM and Notion in one line. Draft hook: "Nobody on this call typed a word." It could be creative 6, or the second-week test once the one-liner A/B on LinkedIn has a winner.

## 4. Competitor to watch: Sembly

https://www.sembly.ai, "AI Meeting Agent for Professional Services": client-facing teams, per-client workspaces, auto-joins Zoom, Meet, Teams and Webex, tasks with owners, branded proposals and decks from meetings, 45+ languages and mixed-language meetings, self-serve 7-day trial, $10 to $30 per user. Same buyer as ours. Its page does not claim voice answers on a video tile, task updates from a later call, each person's own tasks by DM, or Slack Huddles and GoTo. Details: [`competitor-teardown.md`](../../growth-kit/outputs/research/competitor-teardown.md) (addendum).

## 5. Market read in one paragraph

Bottom-up B2B: individuals install free note-takers and teams pay (Fathom: free for individuals, "more than $30M of annual revenue" from teams). Creating tasks from a call is no longer unique (Teams Facilitator, ClickUp AI Notetaker, Mina). What no page we read claims: updating the task in the team's own tracker when someone says "done" on a later call, each person's own tasks with the quote, on six platforms. Class actions against Otter and Granola and company bot bans point Octo at small teams without an IT department, which matches the ICP. ([`market-landscape.md`](../../growth-kit/outputs/research/market-landscape.md))
