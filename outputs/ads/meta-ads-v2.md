# Meta ads v2: the main channel

**Budget:** €700 over 7 days: €500 cold, €200 retargeting from day 3. **Objective:** leads or landing page views (leads once Grona or the page has a form). **Placements:** Reels and Stories first, then Feed. **Format:** vertical 9:16 video, 15 to 36 seconds, captions burned in (most people watch muted). **Destination:** https://octo-landing-47q.pages.dev with `?utm_source=meta&utm_medium=paid&utm_campaign=whos-doing-what&utm_content=<creative>`.

Replaces [`meta-ads-v1.md`](meta-ads-v1.md). Audience and copy follow [`brief/campaign-brief.md`](../../brief/campaign-brief.md).

## Audiences

- **Cold:** ages 25 to 55. Interests: ClickUp, Asana, Trello, Notion, Monday.com, project management, small business, entrepreneurship. Job titles where Meta has them: Founder, Owner, CEO, Operations Manager, Agency Owner. Countries: Portugal, Spain, UK, Ireland, Netherlands, Germany, Nordics.
- **Lookalike, from day 4:** 1% of landing visitors, once there are 100 or more.
- **Retargeting, from day 3:** landing visitors in the last 14 days, and people who watched 50% or more of any creative. Exclude anyone who requested access.

## Five creatives

Each one is a separate ad, so Meta can find the winner. Hook = the first 2 seconds, on-screen text.

### 1. The demo (proof)
- **Video:** `assets/octo-demo-36s-9x16.mp4` as it is.
- **Hook:** "This meeting bot talks back."
- **Primary text:** Every call ends with "so who's doing what?" Octo answers before you hang up, then sends everyone their tasks.
- **Headline:** Your AI teammate on every call
- **CTA button:** Sign up (landing CTA: Invite Octo to your next call)

### 2. The junior PM (the idea)
- **Video:** a 15-second cut of the demo. Open on a founder's face (stock or team member), text over: "No PM? No problem." Then Octo's tile with the task list, then the task message.
- **Hook:** "Your team doesn't have a project manager. Now it does."
- **Primary text:** Octo joins your calls like a junior PM: it tracks who promised what, sends each person their tasks, and keeps ClickUp or Asana up to date.
- **Headline:** The PM your team doesn't have yet
- **CTA button:** Sign up (landing CTA: Invite Octo to your next call)

### 3. Note-taker vs Octo (the comparison)
- **Video:** split screen, 15 seconds. Left: a long AI summary email scrolling, label "Your note-taker". Right: Octo's task message with the exact quote, label "Octo".
- **Hook:** "Your note-taker writes it down. Octo does it."
- **Primary text:** Granola, Fireflies and Otter are great at notes. Octo is built for what happens next: tasks to every person, with the quote they came from, straight into your tracker.
- **Headline:** Note-takers record. Octo acts.
- **CTA button:** Learn more
- **Watch out:** don't say note-takers are silent or can't make tasks. See [`outputs/website/comparison.md`](../website/comparison.md).

### 4. The quote (the receipt)
- **Video or static:** close-up of the task message: "Finish landing page copy · due Friday · 'I can get the final draft by Friday'". Then Anna's face (made-up person), then a check mark when she says "done" on the next call.
- **Hook:** "No more 'I never said that.'"
- **Primary text:** Every task Octo sends comes with the exact words from the call. And when someone says "done" on the next call, it's done in your tracker too.
- **Headline:** Every task comes with a receipt
- **CTA button:** Sign up (landing CTA: Invite Octo to your next call)

### 5. Founder to camera (UGC style)
- **Video:** Oleksii or a team member, selfie camera, 20 to 30 seconds, no editing beyond captions.
- **Script:** "I run a small team and we don't have a project manager. Every call ended with 'so who's doing what?' and then nothing happened. So we built Octo. It joins the call, you can ask it things out loud, and after the call everyone gets their own tasks with the exact words they said. It's on our calls every day. Early access is open, link below."
- **Hook:** "We don't have a PM. We have Octo."
- **Headline:** Get early access to Octo
- **CTA button:** Sign up (landing CTA: Invite Octo to your next call)
- **Before posting:** the speaker checks every line is true for them.

## What to measure

- **Hook rate** (3-second views divided by impressions): is the first frame stopping the scroll? Pause anything under about 25% (*assumption*) after €50 spent.
- **Cost per landing view and per early-access request**, by `utm_content`.
- **50% video views:** these feed retargeting.

## For the creatives person

- Captions on every video, white on a dark box, large.
- Octo's tile and the task message are the two frames that explain the product. Get them on screen in the first 5 seconds.
- People in the demo are made up. Never show a real client's name, call or tracker.
- No em dashes in the copy, no invented numbers, no "free" or "no credit card" (there's no self-serve plan yet).
