# LinkedIn ads v1

**Budget:** €400 over 7 days. **Objective:** website visits (switch to leads once the Grona page has a form). **Destination:** the landing page, with UTM `?utm_source=linkedin&utm_medium=paid&utm_campaign=octo-early-access&utm_content=<ad>`.

## Targeting (primary audience)

- **Job titles:** Founder, Co-Founder, Managing Director, CEO, Head of Operations, Operations Manager, Delivery Director, Head of Delivery, Account Director, Project Manager.
- **Industries:** Advertising Services, Marketing Services, Design Services, IT Services and IT Consulting, Software Development.
- **Company size:** 11 to 50.
- **Locations:** Portugal, Spain, UK, Ireland, Netherlands, Germany, Nordics (English-speaking business audiences).
- **Exclude:** job seekers, students.
- **Audience expansion:** off.

Three ads, same targeting. Pause the weakest after 3 days.

---

## Ad 1: the client's request

**Hook it tests:** losing what the client said.

**Intro text (first 150 characters show before "see more"):**
Your client asked for it on Tuesday's call. Did it make it into ClickUp?

Octo joins your calls as a teammate. When the call ends, everyone gets their own tasks, with a deadline and the exact quote they came from, and your tracker is already updated.

**Headline:** The call ends. The tasks are already in your tracker.
**CTA button:** Request demo
**Creative:** `assets/octo-demo-36s-16x9.mp4` (video ad), first frame: Octo's tile on the call.

## Ad 2: note-taker vs teammate

**Hook it tests:** the category contrast.

**Intro text:**
Note-takers record the meeting. Octo takes part in it.

Ask it on the call, "Octo, what's still open from last week?", and it answers out loud and shows the list on screen. After the call, it does the follow-up.

**Headline:** An AI teammate on every call, not another note-taker
**CTA button:** Learn more
**Creative:** `assets/screenshots/octo-tile-status.png` (single image).

---

## Ad 3: who's doing what

**Hook it tests:** the moment every agency knows.

**Intro text:**
Every call ends with "so who's doing what?"

Octo answers that before you hang up. Each person gets their tasks by message, the tracker updates itself, and next week's call starts with what's still open.

Works on Google Meet, Teams, Zoom, Slack Huddles, Webex and GoTo.

**Headline:** Stop ending calls with "so who's doing what?"
**CTA button:** Request demo
**Creative:** `assets/screenshots/octo-tile-captured.png` (single image).
