# UGC video source

- `build-octo-narrates.js`: builds "Octo narrates" (Octo tells, in first person, what it did on the team call; about 23 s) from `octo-vo-brodie.wav` + `octo-vo-brodie.words.json`, the real demo `assets/octo-demo-36s-9x16.mp4`, the Octo poses and the static end cards. Run from the repo root: `NODE_PATH=$HOME/node_modules node outputs/creative/meta/video/ugc/source/build-octo-narrates.js`. Writes `../octo-narrates-9x16.mp4`, `../octo-narrates-4x5.mp4`, `../octo-narrates-<fmt>-poster.png`, `../contact-octo-narrates.png` (9:16, 2 fps, red lines at y 250 and y 1250) and `../contact-octo-narrates-4x5.png`. Framing, background and type follow `../../source/` and `outputs/creative/design-spec.md`. Demo audio is muted; the VO is limited and normalized to -14 LUFS. The demo's quote line has an em dash before "Anna": the script patches it in source space (white-out plus "Anna, 10:34 · transcript" re-set in Inter at the original size and baseline) and keys the demo's purple page background to the window ink in the message shots. Encoded at CRF 18. The words file runs up to 0.3 s early in the second half, so the script re-times it against the pauses it measures in the wav, refuses to build if a cut lands on speech, and keeps every caption (max 4 words) inside its shot, ending one frame before the cut.
- `build-creator-ugc.js`: builds the creator UGC ad (AI-avatar presenter `creator-body-v1.mp4` + the real demo `assets/octo-demo-36s-9x16.mp4` with its own audio + `outputs/creative/meta/static/end-card-9x16.png`; 27.3 s, 9:16, CRF 18). Run from the repo root: `NODE_PATH=$HOME/node_modules node outputs/creative/meta/video/ugc/source/build-creator-ugc.js`. Writes `../creator-ugc-9x16.mp4`, `../creator-ugc-9x16-poster.png`, `../review-frames/` (every frame at 4 fps, 540 px) and `../contact-creator-ugc.png` (red lines at y 250 and y 1250). Edit: creator (12 % zoom, handheld drift; pauses removed with jump cuts that enter 2 frames before the audio, alternating a ~3.5 % punch-in) until "Watch.", hard cut to Mark's question and Octo's answer on its whole tile (demo 4.68 to 12.16 s, on the spec background), hard cut back to the creator, split screen (task DM, then Anna's quote, then the "updated in tracker" tile) under "everyone gets their own tasks ... ClickUp or Asana.", hard cut to the creator, dissolve to the end card. Captions are hand-timed blocks of 2 to 4 words (max 19 characters) that end one frame before each cut (the script refuses to build otherwise); "AI-GENERATED PRESENTER" stays at x 96, y 282 and moves above "Octo by Octocrew" on the end card. Room tone under everything, two-pass loudnorm to -14 LUFS.
