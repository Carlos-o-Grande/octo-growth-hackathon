FAQ

Questions about Octo

Who is it for?

Small teams of up to 50 people without a project manager, from remote agencies to startups. On your calls, Octo works like a junior project manager that tracks who’s doing what.

How is this different from a note-taker?

A note-taker records and summarises. Octo takes part: it answers questions on the call, shows things on screen, and after the call turns what you agreed into tasks in your tracker and keeps them up to date.

Does it talk all the time?

No. It joins visibly as a participant and speaks only when you call it by name.

Which platforms does it work on?

Google Meet, Microsoft Teams, Zoom, Slack Huddles, Webex and GoTo.

Which trackers does it work with?

ClickUp, Asana, Notion, Trello and others. Tell us yours when you invite Octo.

How much does it cost?

One price for the whole team, not per seat. Plans start at $49 a month with credits included, or pay as you go from $20. See pricing.
