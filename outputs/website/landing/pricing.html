Questions about pricing

Why not per seat?

Because the whole team is on the call. Octo's cost depends on how many calls it joins, not on how many people are in them. So you pay for usage, and everyone shares it.

What uses more credits?

Talking and thinking. A call where Octo mostly listens and sends tasks afterwards uses fewer credits than one where you ask it many questions and it shows answers on screen. The 300 credits per hour is an average.

What happens when I run out?

Octo keeps working if you have a pay-as-you-go balance. If not, we let you know before your credits run out, so you can top up or move up a plan.

Do unused credits roll over?

Plan credits you don't use roll over for one month. Pay-as-you-go balance stays valid for 12 months.

Can I switch plans?

Yes, any month, up or down. You can also cancel at the end of any month.

Can I start today?

Octo is in early access, so there's no self-serve checkout yet. Invite Octo to your next call, and we'll set it up with your team and agree the plan before anything is charged.
