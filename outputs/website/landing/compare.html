Octo vs note-takers

Note-takers give you notes. Octo gives everyone their tasks.

Granola, Fireflies, Otter and Fathom are good at writing the meeting down. Octo takes part in the call and does the follow-up: each person gets their own tasks with the exact quote, and ClickUp, Asana, Notion or Trello stays up to date.

Which one fits you

Notes with no bot in the room

Granola writes notes from your device audio, so nothing joins the call.

A free recorder with summaries

Fathom has a free tier with summaries and action items.

Transcripts and voice Q&A

Fireflies and Otter record the call and answer on a wake word.

The follow-up, done

Octo takes part, sends each person their tasks with the exact quote, and keeps your tracker current from call to call.
