Octo vs note-takers

Note-takers give you notes. Octo gives everyone their tasks.

Granola, Fireflies, Otter and Fathom are good at writing the meeting down. Octo takes part in the call and does the follow-up: each person gets their own tasks with the exact quote, and ClickUp, Asana, Notion or Trello stays up to date.
