# Octo vs note-takers: comparison for the landing page

For note-taker switchers, the comparison explains Octo faster than any headline. Use the short version as a landing section and in ads; the full table backs it up.

**Before anything goes live:** every competitor cell comes from vendor pages and third-party reviews checked on 7 to 8 Nov 2026 (sources below). Re-check a cell before publishing it. A wrong claim about a competitor is the fastest way to lose trust, and can draw a complaint. Where we found nothing, the table says "Not found", not "No".

## Landing section (copy, ready to paste)

**Heading:** Note-takers give you notes. Octo gives everyone their tasks.

**Sub:** Granola, Fireflies and Otter are great at writing the meeting down. Octo is built for what happens next.

| | Note-takers | Octo |
|---|---|---|
| On the call | Record, some answer on a wake word | Takes part: answers by name, shows lists and tables on its own video tile |
| After the call | A summary and action items | Each person gets their own tasks, with a deadline and the exact quote |
| Your tracker | Export or integration, if you set it up | Tasks land in ClickUp, Asana, Notion or Trello |
| Next week's call | Starts from scratch | Knows what's still open, and closes tasks when someone says "done" |

**Honest line under the table:** Want notes with no bot in the room? Granola does that well. Want your team's follow-ups done? That's Octo.

## What not to claim

- That note-takers are silent. Fireflies and Otter both answer on a wake word.
- That nobody else creates tasks. Fireflies, Otter and Fathom all produce action items.
- Any number about time saved or tasks closed. We have no public metrics.

What only Octo claims, as far as we found: it takes part on any of six platforms, each person gets their own tasks with the exact quote, and tasks close when someone says "done" on the next call.
