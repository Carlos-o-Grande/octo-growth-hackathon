# YouTube creators for Octo sponsored integrations (v1, 8 Nov 2026)

Method: subscriber counts were read from each channel's YouTube page on 8 Nov 2026. Upload dates come from each channel's public RSS feed. Sponsor evidence comes from video descriptions (disclosure lines such as "sponsored by", "I partnered with", "(Sponsored)", or affiliate links). "Not found" means I checked the latest ~10 to 15 uploads and saw nothing. It does not mean the creator never does sponsorships. Every channel listed posted in the last 3 months, except where noted.

## 1. Top 5 to contact first

- **Joseph Chandler** (9.76K subs). He is small, so likely affordable, and his "5 Best AI Note Taker Apps for Meetings in 2026" has 53,966 views. A meeting tool already pays him (Fellow sponsored his Aug 2026 Fellow vs Fireflies video), and his current topic is AI agents for small business. This is the closest audience for the least money.
- **Phil Pallen** (75.7K subs). On 22 Oct 2026 he did a paid integration for Viktor, an "AI employee that lives in Slack and Microsoft Teams", which is nearly Octo's pitch. He runs a branding agency, ranks AI meeting assistants (13.6K views), and posts 2 to 3 partner videos a week. His rate is probably above budget, so lead with an affiliate or rev-share offer.
- **George Vlasyev** (43.1K subs). He posts daily on business software for entrepreneurs (ClickUp vs Monday vs Asana, Notion, CRMs). He tested 5 note-takers in a real Google Meet call (39,729 views). He already uses affiliate links (Fireflies, Pipedrive), so an affiliate deal fits how he works. His channel description has a business-inquiries line.
- **Efficient App (Alex & Andra)** (32.4K subs). They compare PM tools and AI assistants: "Muse vs Grok Bot vs Dots vs Instinct" (29 Oct 2026), a Granola 365-day review (27.6K views), and "No bot meeting recorder" (Oct 2026). They run on affiliate links and label videos "not sponsored", so offer an affiliate deal plus free access, not a flat fee.
- **AI Unboxed** (86.4K subs, only 29 videos). Every video reviews one AI tool. In the last month they covered Lindy ("The Ultimate AI Teammate for Work?") and Granola ("AI Meeting Assistant That Never Joins as a Bot"). A collaboration contact is listed in the channel description. They are a good fit for a full dedicated review if a 60-second integration is not possible.

Backups: **Be Productive** (58.2K) sells sponsored SaaS reviews and has the most meeting-assistant videos. **Simpletivity** (411K) has the best audience match (overwhelmed business owners) but likely costs more.

## 4. Outreach approach for a tiny budget

**Proposals, not agreed terms.** The affiliate share, discount and fee below need Oleksii's yes before anyone offers them. Nobody contacts a creator before that.

1. Start with the 5 small and mid creators above. Offer free team access for their own team plus a 30% recurring affiliate for 12 months. Offer a flat fee (EUR 150 to 300) only to creators under ~50K subs.
2. Ask for a 45 to 60 second integration where Octo joins one of their real calls on screen: someone calls it by name, it answers, and the list appears on its video tile. End with the per-person task message. Do not use a slide.
3. Pitch against what they already reviewed: "You ranked Granola, Fireflies and Fathom. Octo is the one that talks back and files the tasks into ClickUp."
4. Ask for one pinned description link with UTM tags, a discount code (for example, 2 months free), and permission to reuse the clip in our own ads.
5. Batch the outreach: send 15 short personal notes the same day, each naming a specific video of theirs. Follow up once after 5 days. Track in one sheet: reply, price, publish date, clicks, signups.
