# Campaign Brief: Tablemate

This is a finished example for a fictional product. Everything in it, including the numbers, the competitors and the event rules, is invented. Use it to see the depth and specificity your own brief should have.

## Deliverable language

English

## Product

Tablemate is an app that lets a small restaurant launch a customer loyalty program in one afternoon, with no agency and no developer.

## Audience / ICP

Independent restaurant owners with 1 to 5 locations. Owner-operators who do their own marketing from their phone between the lunch and dinner rush. They know their regulars by face, but have no way to reach them outside the restaurant. Trigger moment: a slow Tuesday, looking at the delivery app dashboard and wondering where the regulars went. Today they use paper punch cards, a delivery app's built-in rewards, or nothing.

## Problem they have

Their best customers drift to whichever place is discounting on the aggregator apps this week. They pay 25 to 30 percent commission for orders from people who were already regulars. A proper loyalty program feels like a 3-month IT project with a POS integration they cannot afford to break.

## Positioning

Category: restaurant loyalty software. The alternative owners compare it to is a generic punch-card app. The one thing only Tablemate does: it goes live the same afternoon with no POS integration, and it works alongside any system.

## Promise

Launch a loyalty program your regulars actually use, before tonight's dinner service.

## Proof

- 214 restaurants live across 9 cities (source: team, verbal)
- Restaurants on Tablemate see repeat visits rise 23 percent within 60 days (source: team, verbal, internal cohort of 6 months)
- Average setup time is 40 minutes, no POS integration required, works alongside any system (source: team, verbal, onboarding logs)
- Free onboarding call, same day (source: https://tablemate.example.com/pricing, fictional)

## Conversion goal

Book a 15-minute demo, on https://tablemate.example.com/demo (fictional). Not "download the app", not "learn more". One button, one calendar.

## Tone

Confident, warm, practical. Sounds like: a friendly regular who runs the place next door and tells you what actually works. Never sounds like: an enterprise software vendor with a "digital transformation" deck.

## Do not say

- "Revolutionary" or "AI-powered": the product is simple, and the claim is not backed.
- "Guaranteed" results or any promise of a specific revenue increase: the 23 percent is an average, not a guarantee.
- Any comparison that names a competitor as bad. Say what Tablemate does, not what they fail at.
- "Free" without saying what is free (the onboarding call, not the product).

## Second audience (optional)

Small restaurant groups with 6 to 20 locations. They care less about setup time and more about seeing every location's results in one place.

## Landing page URL

https://tablemate.example.com (fictional, put your real landing page here)

## Competitors

- https://punchcard-loyalty.example.com (fictional punch-card app, generic, no reporting)
- https://loyalty-suite-pro.example.com (fictional enterprise loyalty suite, built for chains of 50+)
- https://stampme.example.com (fictional global stamp app, no local onboarding)

## Constraints (optional)

- Event: a fictional 48-hour hackathon. Submission is due Sunday 12:00.
- Judging criteria (example): clarity of the promise 25 percent, evidence behind the claims 25 percent, quality of the assets 25 percent, how well the pieces work together 25 percent.
- Pitch: 5 minutes on stage, including a 1-minute live demo, then 3 minutes of questions.
- Budget: no paid spend. Channels in: landing page, Meta ads, email. Channels out: TikTok.
