# Campaign Brief

Fill in every section. Short and specific beats long and vague. See `brief/example-brief.md` for the depth expected. Fastest path: `use product-scout` (if you have a URL or product name) then `use brand-strategist`.

## Deliverable language

English

## Product

What is it, in one sentence a stranger would understand?

## Audience / ICP

Who exactly? A role or life situation, their trigger moment, what they use today instead.

## Problem they have

What is going wrong in their day that sends them looking for a solution?

## Positioning

Category it competes in, the main alternative buyers compare it to, and the one thing only this product does.

## Promise

The one claim. One sentence.

## Proof

Evidence, most credible first. Each item with its source (URL, file in data/, or "team, verbal").

## Conversion goal

The ONE action and the exact page where it happens.

## Tone

Three usable adjectives, one brand it should sound like, one it must never sound like.

## Do not say

Words, claims or comparisons that are banned.

## Second audience (optional)

Only if the page speaks to a second group. What changes for them?

The page this campaign points at. [TO ADD] if none yet.

## Competitors

Up to 3 URLs. [TO ADD] if none yet.

## Constraints (optional)

Budget, timeline, channels in or out, event rules or judging criteria.
