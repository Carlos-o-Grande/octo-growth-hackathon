## What changed from the agency draft

- **Audience:** small teams of 1 to 50 with no project manager yet (founders, small agencies and studios, startups, service businesses), not agencies only. Every post now names the founder who chases follow-ups, and carries the angle "The project manager your team doesn't have yet."
- **URL and UTMs:** `https://octo-landing-47q.pages.dev`, `utm_campaign=octo-free-trial`, `utm_medium=social`, `utm_source=linkedin|instagram`. `utm_content` ids kept, except LI-5 (see below).
- **CTA:** "Start free trial" plus "Our team sets it up." in every post and on the carousel end slide.
- **Removed:** every "quiet until you call it" and "speaks only when called" line (unconfirmed in the brief). LI-5 was built on it: it is now about who decides which calls Octo joins, and its `utm_content` moves from `li-org-quiet` to `li-org-join` so the old id does not mislabel the post in analytics.
- **No numbers on any post or visual** (design spec): the carousel hook "3 things update after bye" became "What updates after you say "bye"", "36-second demo" became "the demo below", "six platforms" became the list of platforms.
- **Hashtags:** #AgencyOps and #agencyowner replaced by small-team tags.

## Rejected drafts

- LI-1, question hook "No project manager? Your calls have one now.": strong, but it is the paid T1 hook and IG-1's; the founder story ("nobody types") is Oleksii's own proof and fits a personal launch post better.
- LI-2, the agency number hook "3 things update after we say bye": the design spec bans numbers on posts and visuals; the pain hook carries the brief's trigger moment (Tuesday to Friday) instead.
- LI-3, contrarian hook "Gemini is not your competition. Your client's platform is.": reads as a jab at Google and needs the body to make sense; the question is the one buyers already ask.
- LI-4, question hook "How many decisions wait for 'let me check'?": adjacent to LI-3's question hook, and invites a number we do not have.
- LI-5, the agency hook "the best AI on a call is the quiet one": built on the unconfirmed quiet behaviour. Replaced by the "nobody chose to let them in" take, which only needs "you choose which calls it joins".
- IG-1, story hook "Nobody on our team types after a call anymore.": already LI-1's hook on the same day; the T1 question names the v2 audience inside the first 125 characters.
- IG-2, question hook "Who updates your tracker after the call?": the pain hook mirrors LI-2 and the brief's trigger.

## Notes for the team

- **Ship first:** LI-1 with the 16:9 demo and `li-launch-cover.png`, on day 1. It is the only post with the product moving, it is Oleksii's own story (the brief's only proof), and it puts the angle and the promise in one post. IG-1 goes the same day.
- **Hook spread:** LinkedIn uses 4 types (story, pain, question, pain, contrarian), no two adjacent days share one; the number type is gone because of the no-numbers rule. Instagram has 2 posts (question, pain).
- **Still to confirm:** "quiet until you call it" is used nowhere. If Oleksii confirms it on real calls, LI-5 can add "It speaks only when someone says its name." as a sixth bullet.
- **Kept out:** CRM brand names, trackers beyond Notion, Asana and Trello, the sales copilot, translation, "joins only when invited", "bot", "AI teammate" and "AI meeting agent", any count of customers, calls or hours.
