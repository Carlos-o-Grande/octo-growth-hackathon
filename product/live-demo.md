# The live demo

The pitch is a live Google Meet call with Octo, shared on screen. The 36-second video is the backup.

## Setup

- Oleksii opens a fresh Meet and invites Octo. It joins as **"Octo Notetaker Agent"**.
- Octo hears what the Meet hears and answers through the speakers, so the room hears it.
- The presenter talks through a headset and mutes between lines, because Octo answers everything it hears.
- Only the team is on the call. If a guest joins, Octo switches to a reduced client mode for the rest of the call.

## The three lines (about 40 seconds)

| # | Say (in one breath) | What the room sees | Wait |
|---|---|---|---|
| 1 | "Octo, show our high-priority tasks in progress, by owner, on your tile." | A page of tasks grouped by person, and one sentence by voice | ~9 s |
| 2 | "Octo, book a 30-minute slot for me tomorrow at 10 a.m. to follow up with the judges." → "Yes." | It asks to confirm, then creates the calendar event | 5–10 s |
| 3 | "Octo, sum up this call in one sentence." | A one-line recap | ~3 s |

**Backup lines:** "Octo, show our sales pipeline by stage on your tile." · "Octo, search the web: what are three growth marketing trends for 2027?"

Both rehearsals on the morning of 8 Nov passed: every line got an answer in English.

## Want to try it yourself?

Ask Oleksii to bring Octo into a call with you. It runs on Octocrew's real company data, so it's a team-only setup.

## What to keep in mind for marketing material

- A good demo moment is **Octo acting on screen**, not just talking: a page appearing on its tile, an event landing in the calendar, a task message arriving.
- Answers take 3–12 seconds. Cut the waits in any video.
- Avoid "think deeply" requests on camera: they take 20–60 seconds.
