## Product

Octo is an AI teammate that joins your team's video calls, answers when you call it by name, and after the call sends everyone their tasks and updates your task tracker.

## Audience

Founders, managing directors and heads of operations at remote agencies and studios with 10 to 50 people. They run many client and team calls a week, on Google Meet, Zoom and Teams depending on the client. The team lives in ClickUp, Asana or Trello. Trigger moment: a client asks "did you get to the thing I mentioned on Tuesday's call?" and nobody wrote it down.

## Problem they have

Every call ends with "so who's doing what?". Someone has to take notes instead of taking part, and still misses things. What the client asked for on the call gets lost before it reaches the tracker. The tracker goes stale the day after, because nobody updates it when plans change out loud. Their AI note-taker sends a summary that nobody acts on.

## Promise

Note-takers record the meeting. Octo takes part in it, and makes sure every task from the call lands with an owner in your tracker.

## Proof

- Live demo: Octo answers by voice on a real Google Meet call, on stage, today.
- The 36-second demo video: `assets/octo-demo-36s-16x9.mp4` and `assets/octo-demo-36s-9x16.mp4`.
- Each person gets their own tasks by direct message, with a deadline and the exact quote from the call the task came from.
- Tasks land in Notion, Asana, Trello and others, and update when someone says "done" on a later call.
- Works on Google Meet, Microsoft Teams, Zoom, Slack Huddles, Webex and GoTo.
- It runs every day on the Octocrew team's own calls.

People who already pay for a note-taker (Otter, Fireflies, Fathom) and are searching for an alternative. They already know the category. What's different for them: they don't need the problem explained, they need "your note-taker writes it down, Octo does it".
