## Likely questions

- **"Isn't this Otter or Fireflies?"** They record and answer on a wake word. Octo gives each person their tasks with the quote, keeps your tracker current, and closes tasks from the next call. See `/compare`.
- **"Why would a client accept a bot on the call?"** We start with internal calls: the weekly team call is the narrow use case. Octo joins visibly and only speaks when called.
- **"Is it real?"** It runs every day on Octocrew's own calls. You just saw it.
- **"What does it cost you to run?"** Credits cover usage with a margin. About 300 credits per call hour is the current estimate.
