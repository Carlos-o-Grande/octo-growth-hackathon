# Likely questions

**"Otter and Fireflies already talk in calls."**
Yes, on a wake word, for Q&A. Octo's bet is what happens *after*: tasks in your own tracker that stay up to date when someone says "done", each person's tasks by DM with the exact quote, voice reviews, on any of six platforms. Whether that's enough is exactly what we want to test this weekend.

**"Zoom and Microsoft will build this in."**
They will, inside their own platforms. Most teams work across several. It's a real risk, and one of the open questions.

**"How do guests feel about a bot that talks?"**
It joins visibly as a participant and speaks only when called by name. Consent, and what it never does, is something to design with the team.

**"What does it cost?"**
There's no public price yet. Note-takers sit around $8–30 per seat, platform agents around $20 per user. Choosing the model is part of the weekend.

**"Is it real? Who uses it?"**
It runs every day on our own team's calls, and we offer it as part of our AI implementation work. A self-serve version is what we'd launch.

**"How is it built?"**
A meeting bot that joins the call, real-time speech, and a language model with tools for your knowledge base, tracker, calendar and the web.
