# The 90-second pitch

Slides: [`assets/octo-pitch-deck.pdf`](../assets/octo-pitch-deck.pdf). Slides 6–7 are backup.

## Flow

| Time | On screen | What happens |
|---|---|---|
| 0:00–0:15 | Slide 1 | Hook |
| 0:15–1:00 | Slide 3 → Google Meet | Three lines to Octo, live (see [live demo](../product/live-demo.md)) |
| 1:00–1:30 | Slide 5 | The ask |

Slides 2 and 4 come in only when there are two minutes.

## Word for word

**Slide 1.** Hi, I'm Oleksii from Octocrew. Every team I know ends its calls with the same question: so who's doing what? Note-takers record the meeting. We built Octo, an AI teammate that takes part in it.

**Slide 2** *(if time).* Someone always has to take notes, agreements slip between calls, and the tracker is stale the day after. AI tools record the call, but nobody acts on it.

**Slide 3 → Meet.** Octo is in this call with me right now. Let me just ask it.

*[Three lines to Octo.]*

**After the demo.** That's the product. It joins any call, answers from your own data and shows it on screen. After the call, everyone gets their own tasks, and the tracker keeps itself up to date.

**Slide 5.** It works today, on six platforms, with your agent's own name and voice. What it doesn't have yet is a launch. This weekend I want to build that with you: who we sell to first, how we stand out from note-takers that are starting to talk, the brand, the price and the growth loop. Every call Octo joins is a channel. If you're a growth marketer, a designer, a content person or a builder, come find me.
